Good to know

Questions, answered.

The quick facts on PixelMusic, its music sources, privacy and getting started. For the full details, see the project documentation.

What is PixelMusic?

PixelMusic is a free, open-source Android music player for your local library and connected music sources, including YouTube Music and Telegram. It is built with Material 3 Expressive and licensed under GPL-3.0. Explore the source and documentation.

Which Android versions are supported?

PixelMusic requires Android 11 or later (API 30+).

Is PixelMusic free, or does it have ads?

It is free and ad-free. There are no subscriptions, and the project says it does not include third-party ad networks or commercial tracking SDKs.

Which audio formats can I play?

Local library support includes FLAC, ALAC, WAV, APE, OPUS, OGG and MP3, along with AAC decoding. Actual playback quality depends on the file and your device’s audio hardware.

Can I stream Spotify music in the app?

Spotify and YouTube playlist links can be imported; Spotify is not listed as a music-streaming source. For streaming, the project lists YouTube Music and Telegram. Imported playlists and third-party services remain subject to their own terms.

Can I listen without an internet connection?

Music files already on your device can be played locally. Streaming, online artwork and lyrics need a connection; cached data may help with playback, but streaming availability offline depends on the source.

Do I need an account to use PixelMusic?

No account is needed to play local files. You’ll need to connect or authenticate with a service if you choose its features—for example, YouTube Music playlists or Telegram audio.

What happens to my data and music library?

The project’s privacy policy says your library is processed on-device and app settings, playback history and playlists are stored locally. It says the app does not sell your data or include ad trackers; when you use online integrations, information is sent to the services you request. Read the full privacy policy.

Where can I download it, and is it affiliated with YouTube or Spotify?

Get PixelMusic from GitHub Releases or F-Droid. PixelMusic is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, YouTube, Spotify, Telegram or other third-party services. Its disclaimer explains that it does not host or distribute media.
